Navigation Bar Menu Reference

What every tab in the top navigation bar does and how to use it:

Roadmap Core Journey

Your personalized, hierarchical curriculum. Displays multi-month phases, weekly goals, and prerequisite skills tailored to your tech domain and proficiency level.

  • View monthly milestones and roadmap completion percentage.
  • Drill down into weeks and days to see specific learning objectives.
  • Click Start My Journey to anchor tasks to real calendar dates.
Daily Hub Daily Study Cockpit

Your active daily workbench for studying assigned concepts and completing tasks.

  • Access curated video lessons with chapter timestamps and official documentation.
  • Check off completed tasks to advance your day progress and earn XP.
  • Unlock and take optional phase assessments once all tasks in a phase are complete.
Interview Questions Interview Readiness

Targeted technical interview practice specifically generated for your domain.

  • Generate 5 in-depth technical questions and comprehensive answers with Groq AI.
  • Covers coding problems, framework fundamentals, system design, and best practices.
  • Explore curated external interview prep links (LeetCode, NeetCode, GfG, etc.).
Tech News Industry Insights

Live technology news aggregated from Hacker News, Google News, Dev.to, and Tavily, classified by topic.

  • Keep up with releases, industry trends, and tech employer developments.
  • Build context and awareness for managerial and cultural interview conversations.
Analytics Mastery & Progress

Real-time analytics engine measuring your placement preparation velocity.

  • Track overall domain mastery percentage and active streak days.
  • View total XP earned and your current skill tier (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
  • Review diagnostic quiz evaluations to spot weak topics needing extra study.
Internships Job Discovery

Curated internship listings fetched in real-time based on your target domain and location.

  • Search by keywords and filter by domain (Web, Cloud, Data Science, DSA, etc.).
  • Open genuine external employer application portals with one click.
  • Confirm tracking when you actually submit your application.
My Applications Job Tracker

Application Tracking System (ATS) for opportunities you have applied to.

  • Only tracks opportunities where you explicitly confirmed submission.
  • Update statuses across stages: Applied, Assessment, Interview, Offer, Rejected.
  • Add interview dates, recruiter notes, and set follow-up alerts.
Floating AI Assistant 24/7 Placement Guide

The circular sparkle widget in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

  • Understands the exact page, domain, and task you are currently viewing.
  • Ask questions about technical topics, roadmap customization, or confusing code concepts.

The 5-Step Placify AI Journey

Follow these 5 steps to maximize your placement preparation from start to finish:

1
Step 1: Sign Up or Sign In

Create your Placify AI account. Signing in unlocks cloud progress synchronization, streak tracking, and access to all personalized roadmap tools.

2
Step 2: Choose Your Learning Domain

Select your desired tech specialization from 8 core engineering domains (Full-Stack Web Development, Data Science & ML, Cloud & DevOps, AI/GenAI, Cybersecurity, DSA, etc.).

3
Step 3: Select Proficiency Level & Setup

Pick your starting point: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced. Review the syllabus topics and choose whether to validate your baseline via the AI diagnostic quiz or start learning immediately.

4
Step 4: Explore Your Roadmap

Review your customized multi-month roadmap. See which concepts are prerequisites for subsequent phases and follow the structured milestone path to placement readiness.

5
Step 5: Master the Daily Hub & Practice

Execute your daily study plan. Watch curated videos, read documentation, check off completed tasks, practice AI interview questions, and apply to curated internships.

How to Get the Most Out of Placify AI

Pro-tips for effective daily learning and placement success:

Context-Aware Chatbot

The AI Assistant automatically detects what page you are on. When stuck on a task, simply open the chat and ask "Explain this task in simple terms".

Maintain Your Daily Streak

Completing at least one task per calendar day keeps your study streak alive and boosts your mastery ranking on the Analytics dashboard.

Honest Application Tracking

Only mark an opportunity as "Application Submitted" after applying on the external employer site. This keeps your My Applications stats completely accurate.

Weekly Mock Interview Drills

Use the Interview Questions studio to generate questions after every learning milestone. Practice explaining your logic out loud before checking the answer.
